**Super Eagles defender Calvin Bassey** has confirmed that negotiations over a new long-term contract with **Fulham** are actively underway, signalling his desire to settle his future at Craven Cottage despite mounting transfer speculation.

Speaking to **TNT Sports** after Fulham's **1-1 Premier League draw** away to Manchester City at the Etihad Stadium, a result that ended the club's **17-match losing streak** against City, the 26-year-old centre-back said discussions with the West London side are progressing positively. Bassey's existing agreement runs until next summer, though the club holds an option to trigger a one-year extension.

His remarks come amid reports linking him with a January switch to **Manchester United** under newly appointed head coach **Mauricio Pochettino**, as well as interest from Serie A side **Inter Milan**. However, the Fulham captain emphasised his contentment under manager **Álvaro Arbeloa** and indicated he is eager to finalise fresh terms soon.

> “Of course [a new contract is something we want to get done quickly]. We are negotiating; we are still talking. It's an amazing place to be, I have settled in nicely, and the manager has put a lot of faith and trust in me. It's nice to be here, and hopefully we will see what happens with the negotiations,” Bassey said.
