**Nvidia Corporation** shares climbed more than **31 percent** in premarket trading on Monday after the chipmaker's board reinstated co-founder **Jensen Huang** as president and chief executive officer, less than two weeks after he resigned.

The rally followed the board's unanimous decision to bring Huang back immediately, reversing its earlier plan to search for a new chief executive after his departure on **November 12**. Huang's return ends a sudden leadership crisis at the world's leading artificial intelligence chipmaker, whose stock had come under pressure amid uncertainty over its direction.

His resignation came after revelations about personal relationships, including allegations involving a businesswoman suspected by US intelligence agencies of spying for China. The allegations raised national security and corporate governance concerns, though China has denied the espionage claims.

After Huang left, Nvidia appointed Chief Financial Officer **Colette Kress** as interim chief executive while the board looked for a permanent replacement. Pressure for his return grew, with President **Donald Trump** publicly urging the board to reconsider. Huang had also said he would be honoured to return if the board believed it was in the company's best interest.

In its announcement on Monday, Nvidia said the board was satisfied with Huang's candour and leadership after discussions with him. It also said senior US government officials had provided input, and the board concluded that his reinstatement posed no national security risk.

> "The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," said **Harvey C. Jones**, chairman of Nvidia's board.

Huang said he was honoured to return to lead the company and thanked the board, Kress and employees for their support during his absence. The sharp premarket rally suggested investors welcomed the restoration of leadership continuity at a company central to the global AI infrastructure boom.
