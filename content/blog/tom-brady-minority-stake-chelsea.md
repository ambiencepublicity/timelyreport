**Tom Brady** has become a minority investor in **Chelsea Football Club**, according to a report by **TMZ**. The seven-time Super Bowl winner has added the Premier League side to his expanding portfolio of sports investments.

The size of Brady’s stake and the amount he paid were not disclosed. **Forbes** values Chelsea at approximately **$4.2 billion**.

The purchase comes weeks after Brady sold his **3.3 per cent** holding in **Birmingham City**, a club in England’s second tier. The report cited **English Football League** restrictions on holding ownership interests in multiple clubs as the reason for his exit from Birmingham.

Chelsea is the latest addition to Brady’s business interests since he retired from professional football. He holds a **five per cent** stake in the NFL’s **Las Vegas Raiders** and an ownership interest in the **Las Vegas Aces** basketball team. He also owns an **E1 boat-racing team** and is a co-owner of the **Las Vegas Night Owls** pickleball team.
