it appears at the top of the post and as a thumbnail on the home page.

## How writing works

1. Create a `.md` file in `content/posts/`
2. Commit it to GitHub (locally or in the browser)
3. Netlify rebuilds and your post is live

> Markdown features like blockquotes, **bold**, and [links](https://example.com) all work.
